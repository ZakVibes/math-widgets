Hooke’s oscillator, squared, is Newton’s orbit

The left plane shows a particle in the potential −E|w|². Squaring every point, z = w², gives a Kepler orbit with energy E and coupling k equal to the oscillator’s energy. Drag in either plane to move the starting point, or use the sliders.
