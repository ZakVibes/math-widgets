Why are these the same?

Both pictures come from the same game: start with a number, apply a rule, then apply the rule to the answer, over and over. What matters is where the numbers end up.

The bifurcation diagram uses the rule x → r·x(1−x). The Mandelbrot set uses z → z² + c.

They look different, but they are the same rule written in different units, like Celsius and Fahrenheit. Translate with

c = r/2 − r²/4  ·  z = r(½ − x)

and one step of either rule is exactly one step of the other. So each r from 1 to 4 matches one c from −2 to ¼, the stretch of the real axis highlighted in the top panel.

Inside a Mandelbrot bulb the numbers settle into a repeating cycle, and the bulb's period is the number of branches in the diagram below it. That is why the branches split exactly where the axis passes from one bulb into the next, and why the period-3 window sits right under a tiny copy of the Mandelbrot set.
